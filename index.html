Who I work with

◆

Professionals & Leaders

You're capable, driven, and good at what you do. And something still feels off. Maybe you're burning out, repeating patterns, or leading in ways that don't feel like you. You want to understand what's happening inside, not just manage it.

◇

People in Transition

A role, a relationship, an identity is shifting. Career changes, divorce, relocation, starting over. The external change is already happening. This work helps you navigate it without losing yourself.

◊

People Going Deeper

You've done the retreats, the therapy, the personal work. Something shifted. Not another peak experience. Something that stays.

What this work actually looks like

Four made-up people. Very real parts. Tap a card for the meeting inside.

↻

The founder after the exit

He sold the company he spent a decade building. Everyone congratulated him. He felt nothing.

The meeting inside

We met the part that had been running him like an operations manual since his twenties, and asked what it was afraid would happen if he rested. It had a very long answer. The next chapter didn't start with a business plan. It started there.

↻

The lawyer who can't stop

Just this case. Just this quarter. She had been saying it for six years.

The meeting inside

Under the exhaustion we found a part convinced that being needed is the only safe way to be loved, and a younger one still waiting for its first day off. She still works hard. The difference is that now somebody's actually driving.

↻

The one everyone leans on

Beloved. Reliable. Running on empty where nobody sees it. Her relationships flowed one direction: outward.

The meeting inside

We met the part that decided, very early, that having needs makes you a burden. It took some convincing. She didn't become less generous. She became reachable, and someone finally got to hold her.

↻

The couple deciding whether to stay

They love each other, they fight about money, and lately they feel like strangers. Which means it was never about the money.

The meeting inside

One has a part that panics when spending feels loose. The other has a part that rebels when anything feels controlled. The parts had been negotiating for years. Nobody had introduced them. Once we did, the marriage question got easier to ask, and safer to answer honestly.

These four are composites. I made the people up. The parts, however, are entirely real, and they book sessions every week.

What we explore

01

1:1 IFS Coaching

We go inside. Together we explore the parts of you that are protecting, pushing, shutting down, or driving too hard. The inner critic, the perfectionist, the one who is always exhausted. You learn to work with them instead of against them. Change becomes possible in a different way.

02

AI Clarity

AI is powerful and also overwhelming. I help professionals get clear on what they want from it, set up simple tools that fit their real work, and meet the inner resistance that gets in the way. Four weeks, and you run your own setup.

03

Sacred Circles

Small groups of people doing deep work together over 8 weeks. There is something that happens when you witness others going through what you thought was only yours. Intimate, structured, and something that working alone simply cannot give you.

04

Couples Coaching

Two people, two sets of parts, one relationship. I help you understand what shows up in conflict and find your way back to each other. You don't have to be in crisis. The same argument on repeat is reason enough.

05

Organizational Programs

Through FINO, I work with the two operating systems every team runs: the visible one made of processes, tools, and meetings, and the one running inside the people. Change is where those two meet.

I came to this work through living it.

I was born in Russia. Moved to Argentina at 13, then to the United States at 20. I put myself through college and a master's degree working two jobs. Then a decade inside startups, always in the room where things were breaking. I burned out too. Slowly, until I didn't recognize myself.

That's when I stopped trying to fix the outside. I had been doing inner work for years. Breathwork, meditation, circles in different parts of the world. When I found IFS, something clicked.

I trained through the IFS Institute, all three levels, and I'm a certified Deep Transformational Coach. Since then I've worked with clients in more than 15 countries. Whatever brought you here, you're welcome here.
