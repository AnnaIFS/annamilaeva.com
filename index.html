Who I work with

People in Transition

Something big is moving. A job you need to leave, or one you're scared to want. A relationship you're deciding about. A degree you can't quite finish, because finishing means choosing what comes next. You don't need someone to decide for you. You need to hear all of you before you do.

Professionals & Leaders

You're good at what you do, and people rely on you. Lately it costs more than it should. You're carrying the team, or the house, mostly alone. The dream job turned into something else, and you're already wondering how not to burn out at the next one. The inner critic calls itself motivation. Mostly it's exhausting.

People Going Deeper

You've done the retreats, the therapy, the personal work. Something shifted. Now you want to integrate it, so that what you saw holds up in your daily life, in how you speak to the people close to you, in how you show up at work on an ordinary Tuesday. The clarity shouldn't be gone by Thursday. This is how it stays.

What this work actually looks like

Four made-up people. Very real parts. Tap a card for the meeting inside.

↻

The one who checked her CV for lies

She finally got the interview. Her first move was to reread her CV, looking for anything she'd made up.

The meeting inside

We met a part that had kept her small since she was the new kid in a new country. Being underestimated felt safer to it. We thanked it, and asked it to sit in the back row for one hour. Then we wrote her story, practiced it out loud, and she walked in knowing what she'd done.

↻

The lawyer who can't stop

Just this case. Just this quarter. She had been saying it for six years.

The meeting inside

Under the exhaustion we found a part convinced that being needed is the only safe way to be loved, and a younger one still waiting for its first day off. She still works hard. The difference is that now somebody's actually driving.

↻

The one everyone leans on

Beloved. Reliable. Running on empty where nobody sees it. Her relationships flowed one direction: outward.

The meeting inside

We met the part that decided, very early, that having needs makes you a burden. It took some convincing. She didn't become less generous. She became reachable, and someone finally got to hold her.

↻

The one deciding whether to stay

Twenty years in. Separate floors. Everyone has advice, and the word "divorce" comes up in every fight and gets taken back the next day.

The meeting inside

One part was sure leaving meant failing. Another was sure staying meant disappearing. Both were scared, and both had a point. Once each one was heard, the question changed. It became "what's the next clear, kind step." Some people leave. Some stay and change how they stay. Either way, it gets chosen.

These four are composites. I made the people up. The parts, however, are entirely real, and they book sessions every week.

I came to this work through living it.

I was born in Russia. Moved to Argentina at 13, then to the United States at 20. I put myself through college and a master's degree working two jobs. Then over ten years inside startups, always in the room where things were breaking. I burned out too. Slowly, until I didn't recognize myself.

That's when I stopped trying to fix the outside. I had been doing inner work for years. Breathwork, meditation, circles in different parts of the world. When I found IFS, something clicked.

I trained through the IFS Institute, all three levels, and I'm a certified Deep Transformational Coach. The operations years come with me. When you need a plan, a budget, or a system for what's next, we build it together in the session. Since then I've worked with clients in more than 15 countries. Whatever brought you here, you're welcome here.
