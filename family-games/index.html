Family Charades

Phone on your forehead. Everyone else acts.

  1. Guesser: phone on your forehead, screen facing out.
  2. Everyone else acts out the card. Sounds, yes. Words, no.
  3. Got it? Tap the bottom half. Too hard? Tap the top to pass.

    Family Dinner Bingo

    Watch the table. Tap what happens.

    1. Everyone opens their own card. No two are the same.
    2. When it happens, tap the square and say it out loud.
    3. Three in a row? Shout “Bingo!” and show your phone.

    Every square is a moment, never a person.

    Questions for the Table

    Easy questions. Real family stories.

    1. Pick a level: Light, Stories, Deeper or New Year.
    2. Read it out and answer. Then hand the phone on.
    3. Anyone can pass. No reason needed.

    Deeper works best with teens and adults.

    Which Part of You Came to Dinner?

    Something triggered you?

    1. Everyone has parts. Part of you loves this. Part of you wants a nap.
    2. Meet the parts one by one. Tap “That’s me!” on yours.
    3. Read the gift it brings. Then try the 30-second reset.

    Which part came to dinner for you?

    1. Phone in the middle. Everyone looks at the faces.
    2. Pick yours in secret. Don’t say it yet.
    3. Count to three. Everyone points at once.

    After the reveal, tap a face to read the gift it brings.
