The Self-Quest Program

Eight weeks. All in.

A structured dive into your inner system. One quality of Self-leadership each week, a weekly IFS session, and a daily practice that keeps the work alive between them.

See the eight weeks Talk to me first

This is not a course.

There are no modules to fall behind on and no videos waiting for you in a portal. It is a coaching relationship with a structure built around it. Eight weeks, a clear route, and both of us fully in it.

Most coaching happens in the hour. This is built around the other 167. Each week you get a session, a practice for the mornings, a page to write on, and a breath practice you learn properly and keep for life. By week eight, the work is not something you go to. It is something you do.

It starts before the first session

When you book, you get your journal and four questions. Nothing to submit. Just let them sit with you for a few days.

What are you wanting most right now for your life?

What do you want to feel more of?

What needs to transform to create that?

Who would you like to become through this process?

Your answers become the intention we hold for the eight weeks. Everything else is built around it.

The route

IFS describes eight qualities that show up when Self is leading. Calm, curiosity, compassion, clarity, courage, confidence, creativity, connectedness. We take one a week, in this order, because each one makes the next one possible.

01
Calm

Landing before we look

Nothing useful happens in a system that feels unsafe. We start by finding out what your body does under pressure, and giving you a way to change that in ninety seconds. We also set your intention for the eight weeks, in your words, written down.

SessionYour stress signature, your intention, and how the eight weeks will run.
BreathLong exhale practice. The one you will still be using in ten years.
DailyTen minutes in the morning. One page at night.
02
Curiosity

Meeting who is actually running the day

The one who pushes. The one who shuts down. The one who manages everyone else's mood before breakfast. This week you learn the difference between being a part and being with it, which is the whole skill this program is built on.

SessionFinding your main protectors and what each one is for.
BreathAlternate nostril, to steady the mind before you go looking.
DailyCatching the moment a part takes the wheel, and writing down what it was protecting.
03
Compassion

Turning toward the part you like least

Usually the critic. Sometimes the one who eats at midnight, or the one who checks email on holiday. We find out what it has been protecting and how long it has been doing it alone. People often expect this week to be the hardest. It tends to be the one where the fighting stops.

SessionMeeting the harshest voice with something other than argument.
BreathHeart-centred breathing, hand on chest, exhale longer than the inhale.
DailyA letter to that part. Unsent, unedited.
04
Clarity

The map

By now you have met enough of your system to see it whole. Who is polarized with whom. Which parts team up under stress. The pattern you have been calling "how I am" for twenty years. Most people have never seen it drawn. It changes what feels possible.

SessionLaying out your system, including the two parts pulling in opposite directions.
BreathA clearing breath for a cluttered head.
DailyDrawing your map. No artistic skill involved or required.
05
Courage

The thing you have been circling

There is usually one. A conversation, a grief, a decision, a year you skip over when you tell your story. We go toward it at your pace and with your protectors' agreement. Nothing is forced and nothing is dragged out of you. This is the week the four weeks before it were building.

SessionApproaching what has been avoided, with permission and pacing.
BreathA longer connected breathwork round, fully guided.
DailyWhat I have not been saying, and to whom.
06
Confidence

New agreements

Protectors do not stand down because you asked nicely. They step back when they trust that someone competent is holding the thing they were holding. That someone is you. This week we make those agreements explicit, one part at a time, and you start collecting evidence that they hold.

SessionRenegotiating roles with the parts that have been overworking.
BreathGrounding, steady rhythm, for the days that try to take the wheel back.
DailyAn evidence log. Every moment you led, however small.
07
Creativity

New moves in old situations

Insight that never changes a Tuesday is entertainment. So we take something real and coming up. The meeting, the conversation with your mother, the message that has been sitting in drafts. We rehearse it. Then you go and do it, and we look at what happened.

SessionRehearsing a real situation from your actual week.
BreathFree breathing with sound. Most people laugh in this one.
DailyWhat would I do here if I trusted myself?
08
Connectedness

Carrying it home

The last week is about what happens when I am no longer in your calendar. We build the practice that outlives the program, cut down to what you will actually keep doing. Then we look at where this lands: your relationships, your work, and how you speak to yourself on a bad day.

SessionWhat you are taking, and the shortest practice that keeps it alive.
BreathA long integration round to close the eight weeks.
DailyA letter to yourself in six months. I hold it and send it back to you then.

What you get

◆ Eight 60-minute 1:1 IFS sessions. One a week, on Zoom, in English or Spanish.
◆ Eight breath practices, taught live. Not a link to a recording. I teach each one in session, we do it together, and it is yours for the rest of your life.
◆ The Self Journal. Your printed companion for the eight weeks, with the prompts that go with each quality.
◆ A daily practice, about fifteen minutes. Journaling, breath, and meditation, matched to the week you are in rather than handed out all at once.
◆ Support between sessions. Write to me when something opens up. That is when most of it happens.
◆ Resources chosen for you. A reading, a practice, a piece of music. Whatever fits the week you are actually having, not a standard bundle.
◆ A letter from you, six months later. You write it in week eight. I hold it and send it back when you have forgotten you wrote it.

Whether this is your eight weeks

This is for you if

  • You have done some inner work and want it to hold up in your daily life, not just in the room.
  • Something is asking for a decision. A relationship, a role, a way of living that has run out.
  • You know the pattern by name and still cannot get out from under it.
  • You want structure. Open-ended is not helping you.
  • You are willing to give fifteen minutes a day for eight weeks.

This is not for you if

  • You are in crisis right now. That deserves a licensed therapist, and I will help you find one.
  • You want to talk things through without changing anything yet. The four-session package is a better fit.
  • The next eight weeks are already full. This works when you have the room for it.
  • You are looking for advice. I will not be telling you what to do.
