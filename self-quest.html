The Self-Quest Program

Eight weeks. All in.

A structured dive into your inner system. One quality of Self-leadership each week, a weekly IFS session, and a daily practice that keeps the work alive between them.

How it works Talk to me first

This is not a course.

It is a coaching relationship with a structure built around it. Eight weeks, a clear route, two people committed to it, and one intention holding the whole thing together.

A week has 168 hours. Most coaching happens in one of them. Self-Quest is built around the other 167. By week eight, you do not need me in the room for it. You can do it on your own, in the middle of a bad morning.

It starts before the first session

When you book, you get your journal and four questions. Nothing to submit. Just let them sit with you for a few days.

What are you wanting most right now for your life?

What do you want to feel more of?

What needs to transform to create that?

Who would you like to become through this process?

Your answers become the intention we hold for the eight weeks. Everything else is built around it.

How the eight weeks move

Here is the shape of the eight weeks. The detail we go through together.

I

Landing

Nothing useful happens in a system that does not feel safe. We start by steadying yours, we write down what you actually came for, and you meet the parts running your day. By the end of these weeks you can tell the difference between being a part and being with it.

II

Going in

The middle weeks are the deep ones. We turn toward the parts that have been managing you, see the whole system instead of one loud piece of it, and go toward the thing you have been circling for years. At your pace. Nothing is forced.

III

Carrying it home

The last weeks are about making it hold. The parts that have been working too hard get to step back, and you try the new way of responding in real situations from your own week. At the end we keep only the practices you will actually keep doing, and those are what you leave with.

Eight qualities, one a week

IFS describes eight qualities that show up when Self is leading your system. Each week of the quest belongs to one of them, with a session, a practice, and a breath technique built around it.

Calm Curiosity Compassion Clarity Courage Confidence Creativity Connectedness
How it actually runs

An adventure, not a tour

I have never run the same eight weeks twice. The qualities are the terrain. Where we go inside each one depends on you, on what you brought, and on what turns up that week.

Some weeks are steady. Some crack open and we stay there for as long as it takes. I follow what is alive in you, which is why I take only a few people at a time.

What holds every week is the same. A session, a breath practice taught live, a page to write on, and someone walking the terrain beside you who has crossed it before.

What a week asks of you

In time, I mean. This is built for people with jobs and full lives.

60

Minutes, once a week

Our session, on Zoom, at a time we fix at the start so it holds the whole eight weeks.

15+

Minutes a day, at least

Breath practice and the day's page. Fifteen minutes will hold it. Give it more and it gives more back, and over eight weeks that compounds. Some mornings you will want an hour, and the page will take it.

✉

Email, between sessions

When something lands mid-week, you write and I answer. The work keeps moving between our sessions, with me in it.

What you get

◆ Eight 60-minute 1:1 IFS sessions. One a week, on Zoom, in English or Spanish.
◆ Eight breath practices, taught live. I teach each one in session, we breathe it together, and you carry it out with you.
◆ The Self Journal. Your companion for the eight weeks, with the prompts that belong to each quality.
◆ A daily practice, fifteen minutes or as long as you want. Journaling, breath, and meditation, matched to the week you are in rather than handed out all at once.
◆ Personal support between sessions. When something opens mid-week, you write and I answer. Some of the deepest turns happen right there, on an ordinary Wednesday.
◆ Resources chosen for you. A reading, a practice, a piece of music, picked for the week you are actually in.
◆ Accountability that means something. I know what you committed to, and I ask. Eight weeks of someone expecting you changes what you follow through on.

Whether this is your eight weeks

This is for you if

  • You have done some inner work and want it to hold up in your daily life, not just in the room.
  • Something is asking for a decision. A relationship, a role, a way of living that has run out.
  • You know the pattern by name and still cannot get out from under it.
  • You want structure. Open-ended is not helping you.
  • You are willing to give fifteen minutes a day for eight weeks.

This is not for you if

  • You are in crisis right now. That deserves a licensed therapist, and I will help you find one.
  • You want to talk things through without changing anything yet. The four-session package is a better fit.
  • The next eight weeks are already full. This works when you have the room for it.
  • You are looking for advice. I will not be telling you what to do.
