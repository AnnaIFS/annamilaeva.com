The part behind it

Why a part of you does this

This protector keeps you moving because stopping feels dangerous. It wanted support so badly, for so long, that it stopped being able to accept any. Rest looks like a risk to it, so it books one more week. It has kept a lot of things standing. It deserves a break it can believe in.

One thing to try this week

When You Slip
From the book
TodayWhen an old habit starts creeping back.
  1. Ask yourself where you might be slipping right now. An old pattern returning, slowly. You probably already know.
  2. Think of one person you would trust to tell you when they see it.
  3. Ask them. Tell them what you are noticing, and that you would rather hear it from them than catch it three months too late.
Why it helps. Slipping is part of the work. Being seen early is what makes it easy to come back.
