What we do

A shared language for the second meeting

Most teams have the talent. What breaks down is what happens inside people when the stakes rise. Someone shuts down. A hard conversation gets avoided for weeks.
We teach your team a practical way to catch those moments while they are still happening, using parts work, the model behind the novel. Then people practise on the situations they are carrying that week. The five people from the book help. “That was my Priya talking” is a much easier sentence to say in a meeting than “I was scared of your feedback.”
