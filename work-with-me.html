The work we do together

Three ways to work with me, and a door for teams. All of them start in the same place.

01

1:1 Coaching

Inner work and outer work, in the same sessions. We meet the parts that push, protect, or shut down. The inner critic, the perfectionist, the exhausted one. Then we build what comes next: a plan with real milestones, the CV, the script for Thursday. When AI can carry part of the load, we set it up together, and we meet the part of you that resists it.

Choose what fits: a single session, four sessions, or Self-Quest, eight weeks all in through the eight qualities of Self-leadership.

Learn more
02

Sacred Circles

Small groups of people doing deep work together over 7 weeks. There is something that happens when you witness others going through what you thought was only yours. Intimate, structured, and something that working alone simply cannot give you.

Learn more
03

Couples Coaching

Two people, two sets of parts, one relationship. My husband Dario and I work with couples together. He's an organizational psychologist trained in IFS. You don't have to be in crisis. The same argument on repeat is reason enough. Sometimes one of you comes first, alone.

For teams

Your team has parts too.

The conflict nobody names. The meeting that goes in circles. Through FINO, I work with teams and companies on both systems every team runs: the processes and tools, and the people inside them. I often invite teams in through my novel, Already in a Meeting.
